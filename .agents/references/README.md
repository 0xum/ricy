# Progressive loading of evidence

1. Follow the routing as a result of [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md).
2. Read the relevant section of the local README; use
   [COMPONENTS](../../docs/COMPONENTS.md) to locate ownership/boundaries and
   [CONTRIBUTION-AUDIT](../../docs/CONTRIBUTION-AUDIT.md) to distinguish ACTIVE,
   RECOVERY, MIGRATE, HISTORICAL, REMOVE, and UNKNOWN, including part-level roles.
3. Use [DEVELOPMENT](../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#historical-reproduction) for current
   source/build selection. [VISUAL IDENTITY](../../docs/VISUAL-IDENTITY.md),
   [REINSTALL](../../docs/REINSTALL.md), the transport kit, and
   [ricy.md](../../docs/ricy.md) are historical records; consult them by subject/date
   and reconcile recipes against the grounding before use.
4. Play evidence with the commands of [DEVELOPMENT](../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).

Do not copy the complete records to skills, do not load private status without need, and do not treat validations of old sessions as current execution.
